  year: 2012
  url: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3480686/
---

The third edition of the US psychiatric diagnostic manual, DSM-III, deleted "hysterical neurosis". The symptoms once grouped under hysteria were reclassified, mostly as dissociative disorders, ending the formal link between a woman's uterus and her state of mind.
