Writers in the Hippocratic tradition put a whole range of women's symptoms down to movement of the uterus. The Greek word for uterus, *hysteron*, gave the condition its name: hysteria. Similar ideas appear in Egyptian medical writing more than a thousand years earlier. The idea that women's bodies and minds were governed by their reproductive organs lasted for more than two thousand years.
