title: Aspirin is shown to prevent heart attacks, in 22,071 men
source:
  title: Final report on the aspirin component of the ongoing Physicians' Health Study
  publisher: New England Journal of Medicine, 321(3)
  year: 1989
  url: https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM198907203210301
---

The US Physicians' Health Study tested whether low-dose aspirin could prevent heart disease in 22,071 doctors, all of them men. Aspirin cut their risk of a first heart attack by 44%. The result shaped advice on aspirin for heart health worldwide, even though no women had been studied.
