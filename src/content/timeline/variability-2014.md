title: The "too variable" excuse is tested, and fails

  title: "Female mice liberated for inclusion in neuroscience and biomedical research (Prendergast BJ, Onishi KG, Zucker I)"
  publisher: Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews, 40
  year: 2014
  url: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24456941/
---

A common reason for leaving female animals out of research was that their hormonal cycles would make results too variable. A review of 293 studies in mice tested this directly. Females tested without tracking their cycle were not more variable than males on any measure, and males were more variable on several. A similar analysis in rats, published in 2016, reached the same conclusion.
