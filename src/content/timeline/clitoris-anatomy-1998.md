Urologist Helen O'Connell and colleagues at the Royal Melbourne Hospital dissected ten female cadavers and found that standard anatomical descriptions were inaccurate. The clitoris is far more extensive than the flat drawing in most textbooks, reaching several centimetres into the body and wrapping around the urethra and vagina. Her later review found that a German anatomist, Georg Ludwig Kobelt, had described it accurately in the nineteenth century. The knowledge had existed, but it had dropped out of the textbooks.
