title: A contraceptive injection for men is tested, then stopped

  title: Efficacy and safety of an injectable combination hormonal contraceptive for men (Behre HM et al.)
  publisher: Journal of Clinical Endocrinology and Metabolism, 101(12)
  year: 2016

---

Results were published from a trial of hormone injections for men, co-sponsored by a World Health Organization research programme and CONRAD. It worked for about 96% of continuing users, but recruitment had been stopped early on the advice of an external safety review committee, after side effects including depression and other mood changes. Even so, more than 75% of the men taking part said they would be willing to use it. Talk to a doctor before starting, changing or stopping any medication.
