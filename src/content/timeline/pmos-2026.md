In May 2026, polycystic ovary syndrome was renamed polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome (PMOS). The 14-year international process, led from Monash University in Melbourne, concluded that the old name wrongly pointed to ovarian cysts and hid the condition's effects on hormones and metabolism.
