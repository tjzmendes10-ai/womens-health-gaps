title: When women are finally tested, aspirin works differently
source:
  title: A randomized trial of low-dose aspirin in the primary prevention of cardiovascular disease in women (Ridker PM et al.)
  publisher: New England Journal of Medicine, 352(13)
  year: 2005
  url: https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa050613
---

Sixteen years after the men's study, the Women's Health Study reported results from 39,876 women aged 45 and over. Overall, aspirin did not lower women's risk of a heart attack or of dying from heart disease, although it did lower their risk of stroke. Only women aged 65 and over saw a reduction in heart attacks. Advice built on men had not simply carried over to women.
