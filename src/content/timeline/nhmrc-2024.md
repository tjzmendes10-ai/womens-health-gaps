The National Health and Medical Research Council (NHMRC) published a statement asking researchers to routinely consider sex and gender in health and medical research, including whether studies collect data from both males and females. The researchers who wrote this commentary welcomed it, but noted that the funding process had no way yet to check whether applicants actually followed it.
