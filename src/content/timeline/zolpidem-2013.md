In January 2013, the US Food and Drug Administration told manufacturers to halve the recommended starting dose of zolpidem (sold as Ambien and other brands) for women, from 10 mg to 5 mg. Women clear the drug from their bodies more slowly than men, so more of them were still impaired the next morning, including when driving. Zolpidem had been approved in the US since 1992.
