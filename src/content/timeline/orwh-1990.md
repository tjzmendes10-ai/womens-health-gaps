In 1990 the US National Institutes of Health (NIH) created an Office of Research on Women's Health. In 1993 Congress passed the NIH Revitalization Act, which required women and minorities to be included in NIH-funded clinical research, and called for large late-stage trials to be analysed for differences between women and men.
