In February 1961 the oral contraceptive pill became available in Australia as Anovlar. For the first time, women could reliably decide whether and when to have children. It needed a prescription, many doctors at first prescribed it only to married women, and it carried a 27.5% "luxury tax". Talk to a doctor before starting, changing or stopping any medication.
