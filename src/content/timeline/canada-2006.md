The Canadian Institutes of Health Research began building sex- and gender-based analysis into its funding policy and its guidance for applicants and reviewers. From 2010, grant applicants had to report how sex and gender were considered in their study design. This was a prompt to think about sex and gender, not a rule that animal studies must include females.
