Transvaginal mesh implants were widely used to treat pelvic organ prolapse and incontinence after childbirth. Many women were left with chronic pain and other serious complications. After a Senate inquiry reported in March 2018, Health Minister Greg Hunt apologised in Parliament in October 2018 to the women affected, acknowledging their "historic agony and pain".
