title: How we got here
description: A timeline of how women came to be explained away, left out of medical research, and slowly written back in.
---

The gaps in women's health today didn't appear by accident. For centuries, women's symptoms were blamed on the womb. In the twentieth century, a well-meant urge to protect pregnancies helped keep women out of drug trials, and laboratory research defaulted to male animals on the assumption that female hormones would make results messy.

Each of those ideas has since been tested, challenged or reversed. This timeline traces how, with Australia's part in the story included alongside the international milestones.
